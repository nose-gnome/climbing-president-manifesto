My vision

A society where everyone feels ready to climb higher.

As a keen climber of ten years experience, I want our society to be more than just weekly sessions. I want it to be a society where we can make friends, improve skill and feel included.

01

More GOATED trips, socials and events

More GOATED socials, trips and events including internal competitions as well as trying some inter-soc and possibly inter-uni events.

GOATED trips to more climbing walls like WhiteSpider.

02

Remove spam in the group chat

I will create my own AI powered moderation bot that will scan messages from new members of the group-chat to verify they are not a spammer.

03

Training to prepare for competitions

Run training sessions to help us climb the ranks in competitions.

I will get training qualifications over the summer holiday to allow me to provide the group with training sessions. I will look to see if it would be financially feasible to hire someone outside the club to help our more experienced climbers excel.

04

Welcome new members

Have beginner targeted sessions with more leaflets around campus to advertise.

Also introduce termly membership to make the group accessible to people want to join later in the year.

05

Run extra sessions on weekends

Run extra bouldering sessions on weekends for signed-off members, giving people more chances to climb and train around their timetable.

06

Build on the community

Introduce traditions and run more decisions through polls

07

Better communication

Revive the Discord and Events will also be timetabled and posted on our social medias to keep people in the loop.

08

Try to increase funding

Organise fund-raising events and lobby the uni to increase funding.

Why me?

I care about making climbing accessible, social, and exciting.

As someone who's been climbing for 10 years, I believe climbing should be accessible and welcoming to everyone. My aim is to help our society grow while having a better quality of climbing session.
