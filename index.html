My vision

A society where everyone feels ready to climb higher.

I want out society to be more than just weekly sessions. I want it to be a society where we can make friends, improve skill and feel included.

01

Remove spam in the group chat

I will create my own AI powered moderation bot that will scan messages from new members of the group-chat to verify they are not a spammer.

For privacy the bot will be run either locally or via an api to a paid for service which doesn't use the messages for training.

Will most likely be local, just depends whether I can make sure it's uptime is consistent.
