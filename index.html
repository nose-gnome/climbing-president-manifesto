My vision

A society where everyone feels ready to climb higher.

I want out society to be more than just weekly sessions. I want it to be a society where we can make friends, improve skill and feel included.

Why me?

I care about making climbing accessible, social, and exciting.

As someone who's been climbing for 10 years, I believe climbing should be welcoming and welcoming to everyone. My aim is to help us grow while keeping the welcoming spirit that makes the society special.

I believe the best society presidents are genuinely invested in their members. My aim is to help RHUL Climbing grow while keeping the welcoming spirit that makes the society special.

Whether you are here to train hard, try climbing for the first time, meet new people, or get outside more often, I want the society to work for you.
